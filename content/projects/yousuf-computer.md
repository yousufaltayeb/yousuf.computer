This site — the one you're looking at right now. I wanted a home on the internet that felt like me: fast, minimal, and a little rough around the edges. I chose Next.js because I'm learning it for my current job and wanted to practice on something real.

Built with Next.js and Tailwind CSS 4, statically exported for speed. The typography is set in Space Grotesk and Departure Mono. Supports both English and Arabic content with proper RTL layout.

The interactive timeline on the homepage uses Framer Motion for spring-based drag physics. Blog posts are authored in Markdown with TOML frontmatter and rendered with remark/rehype.

Dark mode follows your system preference — no toggle, no fuss.
