## Design Context

### Users

The primary audience is technical recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads evaluating Yousuf for full-stack and backend engineering roles. They are often scanning quickly and need to understand his specialization, evidence of production work, and how to contact him. A secondary audience is other engineers and potential collaborators reading his projects and technical writing.

### Brand Personality

Technical, direct, and quietly distinctive. The voice should feel candid and human rather than corporate or over-polished. It should communicate confidence through specific work and clear language, while preserving the humor and personal details already present in the timeline and writing.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the current fast, minimal, bilingual portfolio: asymmetric editorial layouts, Space Grotesk and Departure Mono typography, the acid accent, system-driven light and dark modes, restrained motion, and the intentionally rough-around-the-edges character. Avoid generic developer-dashboard styling, decorative technology logos, résumé-template layouts, and fashionable effects that weaken the site's individual identity.

### Design Principles

1. Make the role, location, focus, and next action clear within a recruiter's first scan.
2. Support claims with concrete production work rather than promotional language.
3. Preserve bilingual identity and personal character; do not flatten the site into a generic portfolio.
4. Keep interactions accessible, keyboard-friendly, responsive, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
5. Prefer concise copy and a small number of purposeful links over exhaustive technology lists.
